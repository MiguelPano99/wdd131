Project Brief: Hotel Durango Express
Purpose Scenarios Design

Site Name: Hotel Durango Express

Project Overview and Requirements

1. Purpose

A hotel that offers the best hospitality services in Durango, where you can find everything you need on this website. The site serves as the main information and booking portal for prospective guests.

2. Key Scenarios / User Questions

What does the hotel offer?

Focus on amenities, room types, and key features (e.g., dining, meeting rooms).

What services does it have?

List complimentary services (Wi-Fi, parking, 24h room service, fitness center).

What's in the area?

Highlight nearby attractions and points of interest in Durango.

3. Design & Style Guide

Color Scheme

  • Primary Orange: #F4320B (Site Identity)

  • Secondary White: #FEEBE7 (Background/Containers)

  • Text Black: #180501 (General Text)

Typography

  • Titles: "Butler" (using Playfair Display)

  • General Text: "Recoleta" (using Lora)
