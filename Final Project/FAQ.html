F.A.Q.

What is check-out time?

It is always 12:00 p.m.

Is it possible to request a late check-out?

Yes, it is possible, but it is subject to hotel availability and may incur an additional charge. Please check with the front desk the night before or first thing in the morning of your departure.

Does the hotel have free Wi-Fi?

Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is free for all guests in guest rooms and common areas. The password and network name will be provided upon check-in.

Is there parking? Is there a charge?

Yes, the hotel has parking, and it is free of charge.

Is smoking allowed in the rooms?

No. By law and for the comfort of all guests, smoking (including e-cigarettes and vapes) is strictly prohibited in all guest rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

No.
