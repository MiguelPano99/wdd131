HOTEL RULES

  • Check-out: Check-out time is always 12:00 PM. If you wish to stay longer, please contact reception.

  • Minors: Those under 18 years of age must be accompanied and supervised by a responsible adult at all times.

  • Guests reserve the maximum number of guests per room.

  • Smoking Smoking is prohibited. A significant fine will be applied for smoking in the room. Please use designated outdoor areas.

  • Pets: Pets are not allowed.

  • Noise: Reasonable silence is requested between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM to ensure the rest of all guests. Parties or loud gatherings in guest rooms are prohibited.

  • Property Damage: Guests are responsible for any damage caused to the room, furniture, or hotel facilities. The cost of repair or replacement will be charged to the guest's account.

  • If your room requires cleaning, please notify reception and leave your key.

  • The hotel will not be responsible for lost items; it will only be responsible for 30 days.

  • Right of Admission: Management reserves the right to refuse accommodation or request the immediate departure of any person who violates these rules or whose behavior is disruptive to other guests or staff.
